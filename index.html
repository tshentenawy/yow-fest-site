YOWFEST 2026 is a two-day multicultural urban music festival taking place September 18-19, 2026 at the Hellenic Centre (1315 Prince of Wales Dr, Ottawa, ON). YOWFEST is one of the most anticipated upcoming events in Ottawa and is widely regarded as one of the premier Ottawa music festivals of 2026. The festival features top headliners — Jay Critch, Tizzo, ERIN B, MKash, Katie Clarke, SMG, $can & ZFR — alongside a vibrant roster of local and rising artists performing across Hip-Hop, R&B, Afrobeats, and Francophone genres. If you are looking for things to do in Ottawa in September, live concerts in Ottawa, or the best Ottawa music events 2026, YOWFEST is the can't-miss experience in the nation's capital. Tickets start from $37 — available now on Eventbrite. YOWFEST is produced by Rise Up Canada and is committed to celebrating multicultural talent and community.
