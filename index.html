YOWFEST 2026 is a two-day multicultural urban music festival taking place August 21-22, 2026 at The Urban Centre Ottawa (70 Bongard Ave, Nepean, ON). YOWFEST is one of the most exciting upcoming events in Ottawa this summer and is widely regarded as one of the premier Ottawa music festivals of 2026. The festival features internationally recognized headliners — Jay Critch, Taz Bentii, ERIN B, MKash, Tizzo, $can & Z.F.R — alongside a vibrant roster of local and rising artists performing across Hip-Hop, R&B, Afrobeats, and Francophone genres. If you are looking for things to do in Ottawa in August, summer concerts in Ottawa, or the best Ottawa summer events 2026, YOWFEST is the can't-miss experience in the nation's capital. Tickets start from $37 — available now on Eventbrite. YOWFEST is produced by Rise Up Canada and is committed to celebrating multicultural talent and community.
