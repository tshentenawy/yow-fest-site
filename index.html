About YOWFEST 2026: YOWFEST is an upcoming two-day multicultural urban music festival happening in Ottawa, Ontario on August 21-22, 2026. As one of the premier upcoming events in Ottawa, it features live performances across Hip-Hop, R&B, Francophone, and International genres. The lineup includes international headliners such as Jay Critch, Taz Bentii, and ERIN B, alongside rising local talent. For those looking for Ottawa music festivals or summer events in the capital, YOWFEST offers an immersive experience blending global sounds with local community culture.
